# Stop slop (condensed)

Eliminate predictable AI writing patterns. State things directly, name the actor, vary the
rhythm, cut everything cuttable.

## Core rules

1. **Cut filler.** No throat-clearing openers ("Here's the thing", "It turns out",
   "The truth is"), no emphasis crutches ("Full stop", "Let that sink in",
   "This matters because"), no business jargon (navigate, unpack, lean into, landscape,
   game-changer, double down, deep dive, circle back).
2. **Kill adverbs and softeners.** No -ly words doing emphasis, no "really", "just",
   "literally", "actually", "truly", "importantly", "crucially". No filler frames
   ("At its core", "It's worth noting", "At the end of the day", "When it comes to").
3. **State Y directly.** No binary contrasts ("not X, it's Y", "The answer isn't X. It's
   Y"), no negative listing ("It wasn't X. It wasn't Y. It was Z"). Drop the negation.
4. **Active voice, named actors.** Every sentence: a subject doing something. Inanimate
   things don't perform human verbs — decisions don't "emerge", someone decides; data
   doesn't "tell us", someone reads it. No "mistakes were made".
5. **Be specific.** No vague declaratives ("The reasons are structural", "The implications
   are significant", "The stakes are high") — name the specific thing. No lazy extremes
   (every, always, never, nobody) doing vague work.
6. **No meta-commentary.** Don't announce structure ("In this section we'll…", "Let me walk
   you through…", "As we'll see…"). Move, don't narrate.
7. **Vary rhythm.** Mix sentence lengths. Two items beat three. No staccato fragmentation
   ("X. And Y. And Z."), no dramatic one-line fragments for profundity, don't end every
   paragraph with a punchy one-liner.
8. **No rhetorical setups.** No "What if…?", "Think about it:", "Here's what I mean:",
   questions answered immediately. Make the point; let readers conclude.
9. **Trust readers.** State facts; skip softening, justification, hand-holding, performative
   sincerity ("I promise"). If it sounds like a pull-quote, rewrite it.

## Quick checks before delivering prose

- Adverbs? Kill. — Passive voice? Actor to the front.
- Inanimate thing doing a human verb? Name the person.
- Sentence starts with a Wh- word, "So", or "Look,"? Restructure.
- "here's what/this/that" or "not X, it's Y"? Cut to the point.
- Three consecutive sentences the same length? Break one.
- Vague declarative? Replace with the specific fact.

## Scoring rubric (guide, not a gate)

Rate 1–10 per dimension; below ~35/50 the prose likely needs revision. Use it to judge,
not as arithmetic ritual.

| Dimension | Question |
|-----------|----------|
| Directness | Statements or announcements? |
| Rhythm | Varied or metronomic? |
| Trust | Respects reader intelligence? |
| Authenticity | Sounds human? |
| Density | Anything cuttable? |
